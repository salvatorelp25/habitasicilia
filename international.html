Your Trusted Property Advisor in Sicily

Located in the heart of the Agrigento province — minutes from the Valley of the Temples and the southern Mediterranean coast — Habita Sicilia helps international buyers acquire authentic Sicilian homes, countryside villas, and high-yield commercial assets with full legal and technical clarity.

Start Your Sicilian Property Search

1. Independent Property Scouting

We do not push overpriced tourist listings. We actively scout off-market villas, historic town houses, and renovation projects in Canicattì, Agrigento, and surrounding countryside based on your exact criteria.

2. Italian Cadastral & Legal Due Diligence

Buying in Italy requires verifying building permits, cadastral floor plans (Catasto), energy certificates (APE), and tax regimes before signing any deposit. We inspect every document upfront.

3. End-to-End Notary & Tax Support

From obtaining your Italian Fiscal Code (Codice Fiscale) and opening an Italian bank account to coordinating bilingual Notary deeds and local surveyors (Geometri), we stay by your side at every step.
